approved Research Desk web outputs. The owner saves, links or dismisses each one.

preserved. Duplicate and concurrent saves do not create duplicate source cards;
dismissed suggestions stay dismissed on recollection. References are not fetched.

Added reviewed application of an approved source-synopsis output to its exact
source card. The owner edits before applying. Only synopsis changes; all other
fields remain. Previous/applied payloads, output hash, project and source version

checked content and canonical project approval are checked before application.

the new review operations makes model requests or updates general memory.

v1.18 source-file checks are retained. No new dependencies or account permissions.
