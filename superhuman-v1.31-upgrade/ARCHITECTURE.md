# Project leadership architecture — stage 1

The existing team project is the parent record. Milestones add business questions, acceptance criteria, contributor rosters, independent reviewers and strategic approval gates. Existing team tasks remain the execution records; existing agentic runs, tools and budgets remain the execution engine.

## Ownership

Owner > The Twin (supervisor) > department specialist (project lead) > approved milestone contributors. A lead's authority is scoped to the project and roster. Coordination decisions use the actual lead identity and its metered budget pool; they are not invented conversations or a generic Twin pretending to be every specialist.

The lead integrates all milestone contributions, even when another specialist leads a particular investigation. The Twin's author contribution, when specified, is saved separately. Independent reviewer output is required for milestones where The Twin contributed.

## Lifecycle

Planned -> Working -> Specialist review (where required) -> Twin review -> Owner review -> Approved.

Working contains contributor execution, lead acceptance and integration. A Twin revision returns the integration to Working and produces a new immutable packet. An escalation becomes Needs owner. Source changes, permission changes, invalid evidence or interrupted requests can hold the project without replaying a paid decision.

Successful task execution is not strategic approval. Only an explicit owner decision on the current Twin-reviewed packet approves a milestone. Successor work remains blocked until then.

## Knowledge and conversations

Storage source grants identify a specific record version, project, named recipients and purpose. Source approval, source sharing, project-output sharing, text extraction and substantive review are separate records. Scope and sensitivity checks apply in existing Storage retrieval and new page readers. Working notes are untrusted reference data, never new owner facts or permissions.

Project conversations use explicit project associations and the approved roster. They inherit the existing private-room protections and exclude global inbox context.

## Review and audit

Packets retain exact integrated text, source-task references/hashes, any Twin contribution hash, export records and version history. Reviews identify their real actor and decision. Canonical and team review routes cannot bypass milestone workflow. Two automatic revision requests are allowed, with explicit owner handling of persistent failure.

The controller reviews complete bounded outputs and rejects oversized contexts. Integrity checks and extraction coverage do not replace factual, commercial, legal or technical judgment. Strategic approval remains with the owner.

## Deferred stage 2

A requirements/evidence register; formal missing-evidence investigations; cross-specialist evaluation rubrics; changed-decision propagation and milestone reopening; initiative-to-project linkage; outcome baselines and impact follow-through. The two previously recorded broader gaps remain deferred: unified Twin conversation/coordination and accepted business-outcome measurement.
