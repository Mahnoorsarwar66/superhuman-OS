# Stage 2 architecture

The v1.31 hierarchy and task engine remain authoritative: owner > The Twin > department project lead > approved specialists. The assurance package adds records and gates, not another scheduler, chat console or independent writer.

## Evidence and quality

A milestone has a versioned scope register, requirements, evidence quotations, research gaps and a rubric. Evidence pins source identity/version, exact quotation, locator, interpretation, classification, proposer and subsequent judgment. Source existence and quotation matching are deterministic checks; relevance and correctness remain semantic judgments. PDF quotations use a named text page, without claiming OCR or comprehension.

An integrated packet freezes the register fingerprint alongside the existing immutable body and task-source hashes. Complete specialist scorecards refer to that packet and fingerprint. A failing assessment may be a correct critique; accepting the critique is separate from accepting the deliverable. The Twin accepts only with passing current independent-specialist and Twin assessments, and still forwards strategy to the owner.

Existing metered lead/Twin decisions receive permitted register context and review standards. Employee tools derive actor, milestone, project and privacy from their assigned execution records; the caller cannot supply those authorities. Page-bounded register reading retains source-access receipts. Public research cannot read private register entries or inherit broader source grants.

## Investigations and changes

An eligible gap creates an idempotent contributor assignment within the approved working team, existing limits and current privacy controls. It joins the integration prerequisites. The lead accepts/revises its actual result through the existing workflow. Supporting gaps may be explicitly deferred by the owner; critical gaps cannot be dismissed as resolved without investigation.

Source freshness/access is checked before work and milestone decisions. A changed accepted source or reviewed scope creates a deduplicated impact record. Dependency descendants are blocked. No automatic process withdraws strategic approval: an explicit owner impact decision reopens the affected work, preserves packet/action history and requires fresh confirmation and reviews. Same-run budgets and ceilings remain; further capacity is an explicit existing control.

## Initiative follow-through

Initiatives link proposals, project signals or project conversation suggestions to existing governed projects. Owner approval is a strategic record, not permission to execute arbitrary work. Outcome metrics retain units, baseline, target, direction and review date. Source-backed observations are proposed before owner acceptance; stale sources do not count as current measurements. Attention shows due follow-ups. Arithmetic change is descriptive and does not establish attribution or return on investment.

## Installation and remaining gaps

Exact-source staged installation, private backups and guarded rollback preserve the earlier deployment model. Rollback keeps assurance data, removes unsupported tools, disables coordination and pauses work. Reinstall respects intervening owner permission changes.

Existing v1.31 approvals are explicitly historical; pending packets must pass the new gates. This release does not claim an actual guide review, live provider audit or measured business result. Unified Twin conversation/coordination remains deferred. The broader business-outcome gap remains open until real projects establish accepted metrics, collection methods and reliable observations; these records provide its foundation. Questionnaires, advanced deliverable generation and external-action execution remain separate work.
